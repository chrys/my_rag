# Product Guidelines - My RAG

## Prose Style
- **Technical yet Accessible:** Write for an audience that understands RAG concepts but appreciates clear, jargon-free explanations.
- **Active Voice:** Use the active voice to make instructions and descriptions more direct and engaging.
- **Consistency:** Maintain consistent terminology across the UI, documentation, and API.

## Branding
- **Professional & Trustworthy:** Use a clean, modern aesthetic that conveys reliability and security.
- **Data-Centric:** Emphasize the user's data and the insights derived from it.

## UX Principles
- **Clarity:** Ensure every action and status is clearly communicated to the user.
- **Efficiency:** Minimize the steps required to upload documents and start a search.
- **Feedback:** Provide immediate and informative feedback for all user interactions, especially long-running tasks like document ingestion.
- **Accessibility:** Design with accessibility in mind to ensure the platform is usable by everyone.
